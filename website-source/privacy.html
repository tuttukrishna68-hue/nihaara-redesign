Your details, considered

Privacy notice.

This notice describes the enquiry features included in this website.

When you get in touch

The enquiry form asks for your name, email address, phone number, project requirements and, optionally, the project location. These details are used to respond to your enquiry, discuss your project and arrange any visit you request. Please avoid including sensitive documents or financial information in the form.

How your enquiry reaches us

The website’s default form sends your enquiry through EmailJS, the email service used by the original Nihaara website. If the studio enables the included server mode, enquiries are stored on the studio’s server and may be forwarded through its configured notification service. A saved enquiry and an email delivery are shown as different states.

When you choose WhatsApp or your email app, the details you entered are placed into a draft message. You decide whether to send it. These services have their own privacy policies and account settings.

Local features

This website does not add advertising trackers or analytics. The form keeps your typed information in the current page while it is open; it does not save it to browser storage. Gallery filters are reflected in the page URL. The included fonts and project images are served with the website.

Your choices

You can contact the studio directly without using the form. For questions about your enquiry, or to ask for a correction or deletion of details you submitted, write to vasthunihaara@gmail.com or call +91 94473 30144. The studio can explain the retention of its project and enquiry records.

External links

Maps, social media, WhatsApp and email service links open external services. Their own privacy and service terms apply.
