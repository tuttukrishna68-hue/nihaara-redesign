01   Studio

Since 2006, we have designed homes across Kerala — rooted in Vasthu, considered in every detail, and made for the way you live.

Nihaara Architects has been a trusted name in architecture and interior design in Kerala since 2006. Our work brings architecture, engineering, planning and landscape design together.

We take pride in resourceful design and novel aesthetics. From the first drawing, we address the technical details that turn a good idea into a place that feels like home.

04   Our approach

From the first sketch
to the last detail.

01

Read the site

Every project begins with listening. We understand your brief, visit the site, and consider its setting before a line is drawn.

02

Plan to Vasthu

Your requirements, the orientation of the site, and the principles of Vasthu inform a layout that works for your everyday life.

03

Resolve every detail

The plan develops into 3D views and detailed drawings. Interiors, materials and finishes are considered together, with Nihaara Micro Details taking the design further.

04

Bring it to life

With construction services and visits at agreed stages, we help carry the design through to the finished space. Scope and fees are discussed with you from the start.
