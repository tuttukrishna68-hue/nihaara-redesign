Who we are

A collaborative practice,
with people at its heart.

Nihaara Architects offers comprehensive design solutions in architecture, interiors, engineering, planning and landscape architecture. We bring technical considerations into the process from the very beginning — from structure and services to materials and sustainability.

Our design solutions grow through collaboration. A multidisciplinary team shares knowledge, researches alternatives and imagines new ways to respond to the built environment. We use design to enrich people’s lives and help organisations succeed.

Our mission is to deliver exceptional ideas through the creative blending of human need, environmental stewardship, value creation, science and art. We consider the wider picture while attending to the details, designing for the ways contemporary life evolves.
